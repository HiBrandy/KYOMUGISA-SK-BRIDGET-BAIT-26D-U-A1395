Our story

QLT Technologies began with one laptop and a simple belief: good software should be affordable, easy to use and built to last. Today we help schools, shops, clinics and farms move their work online.

We are a small, focused team. That means you work directly with the people building your product, and you always know where your project stands.

"Small steps every day make big software." — Amina K., Founder

Our mission

How we work

Four simple steps from idea to launch.

  1. Plan
    We listen and write down exactly what you need.
  2. Design
    We sketch the screens and agree them with you.
  3. Build
    We write, test and improve the code.
  4. Launch
    We go live and train your staff.
